    Safety

    This app cannot call for help for you. It does not detect crashes or send alerts. Set it up before you ride, and do not use your phone while the bike is moving.

    Before you ride

    • Helmet on and fastened, with protective gear
    • Tyres, brakes, chain and lights checked
    • Phone fixed in its mount, and the ride started before you move off
    • Enough fuel, and someone knows where you are going

    If something goes wrong

    • Get yourself and the bike somewhere safe, away from traffic
    • Call 112, India's national emergency number, or the local emergency number if you are elsewhere
    • Say exactly where you are, using a road name or a landmark
    • Do not ride on if you are hurt or the bike is not safe

      About GPS figures

      Speed comes from your phone's GPS. Distance is an estimate: readings less accurate than 50 m are ignored, and small drifts while standing still are not counted. It is not your bike's odometer.
